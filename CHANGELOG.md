# Changelog

What changed in each version, as people see it. Each version's part becomes the notes of its release.

## 1.2.11

- A dictation in Claude (and other apps that refresh their text field while you type) goes into the field again instead of waiting as Insert here.
- The keyboard's top strip gives as a rubber band both ways: pulled up, the keys stretch apart a little; pulled down, they squeeze together a little; let go, they spring back.

## 1.2.10

- Show original in History's ⋯ shows again only where the text was changed from what you said.
- The app above the keyboard no longer jumps when the keyboard opens.
- Style and Translate open their list only on a tap or a hold: a finger sliding down off them no longer opens it and picks a row by chance.
- A tap on the recording dot stops the recording also when it was started from the keyboard in another app; Show keyboard stays in the dot's menu.
- The keyboard offers to paste only a copied image or a screenshot; copied text waits in the clipboard without asking.
- A paused dictation says what × does: Paused — press × to continue later.

## 1.2.9

- × on a paused dictation keeps it in History instead of throwing it away; from its ⋯ there, Continue recording records on into it.
- Show original sits in every dictation's ⋯ in History, greyed where nothing was changed from what you said, instead of showing on some rows only.
- While a dictation is processed, the water in the mic and in the floating dot freezes where it was instead of sinking.

## 1.2.8

- The keyboard no longer jumps when you pull it down by its grabber: the keys go down with it instead of snapping back up.
- A dictation goes straight into the field again after Retry, and after the floating dot switches to Waveei's keyboard, instead of waiting behind Insert here.
- The blue dot on the clipboard key lights only for a recording not transcribed yet, no longer for every copy or finished text.

## 1.2.7

- The keyboard comes back to the app you left for 2 minutes, now with the floating icons turned off too.
- The recording ring sits a little further out from the mic, looks crisper and shows sooner after the touch.
